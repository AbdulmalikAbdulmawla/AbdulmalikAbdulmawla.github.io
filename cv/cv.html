Profile

I come from research and have been building tools for many years that help solve spatial problems: for the design, generation and analysis of spatial data. I believe that any complex challenge, and any handling of data, requires intelligently breaking the problem down to its core parameters and simplifying each element until a humanly manageable scale is reached; this is how nature works: complex systems are not made of complex parts, but of very simple ones, intelligently put together. Technology is one of my strengths; my main one is this computational thinking about spatial data problems, together with the capacity to work my way into the field the data serves, from simple to complex objectives. In doing so I work on the systems behind the tools, from agent-based simulation to AI-assisted, multi-agent coding.
