Profile

I come from research and have been building tools for the design, generation and analysis of spatial data for many years. Technology is one of my strengths; my main one is computational thinking about spatial data problems: I break processes down into their elemental parts, translate them into parametric, interactive environments and work my way into the field the data serves, from simple to complex objectives. In doing so I work on the systems behind the tools, on data models, methods and interfaces, with state-of-the-art technologies: from agent-based simulation to AI-assisted, multi-agent coding. My standard is the best balance between user experience and advanced scientific analysis methods; I see early where the users of a tool will stumble.
